# Code of Conduct

This project is technical reverse-engineering research. Discussion can be direct and critical, but
contributors are expected to keep it professional and evidence-focused.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code, claims and evidence — not people.
- Separate observation from interpretation.
- Credit prior art and original researchers.
- Correct mistakes openly when better evidence appears.
- Respect privacy: do not post VINs, addresses, credentials or identifying navigation data.
- Do not pressure contributors to disclose proprietary material they cannot lawfully redistribute.
- Be specific when disagreeing: firmware, binary hash, function, log, trace or reproduction steps are
  more useful than authority claims.

## Not acceptable

- harassment, insults or personal attacks;
- doxxing or publishing identifying information;
- knowingly misrepresenting someone else's work as your own;
- distributing credentials or private keys;
- posting proprietary source/binaries without the right to redistribute them;
- using Issues/Discussions primarily for commercial solicitation or spam.

## Technical disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is welcome.

When evidence conflicts, prefer this order:

1. reproducible vehicle observation;
2. exact binary/source evidence;
3. reproducible build/test;
4. documented prior art;
5. hypothesis.

A newer, stronger observation may supersede an older project conclusion.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove contributions that violate these rules or expose sensitive
information.

Security-sensitive findings should follow `SECURITY.md` rather than being posted as a normal public
Issue.
